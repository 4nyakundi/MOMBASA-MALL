The highest-traffic intersection in the mall, directly flanked by Naivas Supermarket and the main avenue entrance. Perfect for automobile displays, major brand showcases, experiential booths, and stage performances.

Open-air prominent street-level activation space facing the bustling Mwembe Tayari roundabout. Ideal for roadshows, promotional gazebos, direct consumer sampling, and live DJ sets.
