Our Story, Journal & Visitor Reviews

Mombasa's premier shopping, dining, entertainment and lifestyle destination — positioned at the heart of Mwembe Tayari along Jomo Kenyatta Avenue, serving the Coast since 2012.

Where Commerce, Culture & Coastal Luxury Converge

Mombasa Mall was engineered as a dynamic vertical urban destination — uniting everyday essentials, tier-1 banking, international retail, vibrant entertainment, and panoramic rooftop dining under one architectural roof.
