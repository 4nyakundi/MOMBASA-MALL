Anchor Retail & Brands

Mombasa Mall is anchored by a sprawling, fully-stocked Naivas Supermarket on the ground floor, serving as the central hub for grocery and household shopping in the CBD. Accompanying it are global fashion chains like Lovisa, Michaels Boutique, and Denri, offering premier retail experiences.

Co-Working & Startups

Hosting the Westerwelle Startup Haus on the third floor, Mombasa Mall serves as a thriving workspace for digital nomads, startups, and tech innovators. This incubator provides high-speed internet, private meeting rooms, and shared workspaces to foster economic growth in Mombasa.

Wellness & Medical Care

Prioritizing the health of our community, the mall features dedicated wellness zones, diagnostics centers, and modern medical clinics. From routine family care to specialized pharmacy access, essential healthcare services are conveniently integrated under one roof.

Family Play & Dining

Experience wholesome entertainment at our Play-On Kids Zone, featuring safe, creative playgrounds and indoor activities for children. Unwind at our premium food court, which features diverse dining spots offering local Swahili cuisine and global fast food chains.
