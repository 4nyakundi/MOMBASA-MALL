Games & Activities

Explore our highly interactive zones designed for fun, strength, and brain training.

Soft Play Area (1-3 Years)

Bounce, slide and laugh safely in our colorful soft ball play area.

Trampoline (4-15 Years)

A trio of high-energy trampolines for kids to jump and play around.

Go-Karting (6-15 Years)

A thrilling indoor go-karting track where kids race for fun and glory.

Tap Wall (4-15 Years)

Perfect for brain activities, reaction timing and whole body movement.

Ninja Course (4-15 Years)

Test agility, climbing strength, balance, and problem-solving skills.

Ballpit & Slide (4-15 Years)

A massive ball pit with a fast spiral slide to dive, slide and play.

Basketball Hoops (4-15 Years)

Practice hoops, jump shots, and challenge friends to shooting contests.

Spider Wall (4-15 Years)

Stick to the wall in velcro suits and try to free yourself from the dungeon.

Zip Lines (4-15 Years)

A thrilling indoor zip line experience letting kids glide through the air.
